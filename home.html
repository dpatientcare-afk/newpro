About Me

Building Solutions at the Intersection of Technology & Strategy

I am Abdulaleem Alsofi, an Information Technology graduate with a deep passion for web development, artificial intelligence, data analysis, and project management. My academic journey at Jand University of Science and Technology equipped me with a strong technical foundation, which I continuously expand through hands-on projects and professional development.

I believe in using technology not just for its own sake, but as a tool to solve real problems, optimize workflows, and empower people. Whether building responsive web applications, analyzing data to uncover insights, automating repetitive tasks with AI, or managing projects from concept to delivery, I bring a multidisciplinary approach to every challenge.

My vision is to grow as a technology and management professional who contributes meaningfully to digital transformation initiatives, while also sharing knowledge through training and mentoring.
